Approach

Hard, not just tedious.

We take on work where a mistake has consequences, where an expert has to sign off on the result, and where the input is messy real-world data. Automating that badly saves nobody time, so we only ship what holds up for the people who do the work today.

01

The expert's standard

We measure our output against what the best practitioners produce by hand, and they review it. If it is almost right, someone still has to redo it.

02

Built with the people who do it

We work alongside the experts from the first week, and their feedback reaches the product within the hour.

03

Every result checkable

The person who signs off can see where each part of the result came from, and correct it before it leaves their desk.

How we work

Engineering run by AI agents.

Our engineering is a team of AI agents, Claude above all, directed by our CTO. Agents write the code, other agents review it, and one stands in for the customer and checks the work against their standard.

We hold them to measurements, not opinions. A change ships when it passes checks against real experts' work, and not before.
